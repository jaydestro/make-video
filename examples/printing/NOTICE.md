# The shapes of letters

`narration.wav` is a **human recording**, not a cloned or synthetic voice.
Linda Johnson read the historical passage on printing in *Arts and Crafts Essays* (1893)
for LibriVox. Keith Ito and Linda Johnson published the aligned excerpts in the
[LJ Speech Dataset, version 1.1](https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/#license).
The publisher identifies the text, recording and annotations as **public domain in
the United States**; check local law for reuse elsewhere. This material is not
relicensed under the code's MIT license. No third-party font or voice model is bundled.
The Arabic subtitle translation and example design are project contributions under MIT.

The WAV concatenates the original mono, 22,050 Hz PCM samples of two adjacent
clips with no retiming, artificial pause or synthesis. The English SRT uses their
published words; line wrapping and the sentence join are presentational. The
Arabic SRT is a translation, not a second narration or a word alignment.
